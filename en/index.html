About Tua Italia

We create stories worth remembering

Tua Italia is a celebration of life, people and extraordinary moments. It was born out of a love for the Italian way of life — full of beauty, authenticity, closeness and the joy of being together.

We believe the most beautiful memories are born from emotion, and every story deserves a setting that lets it truly resonate. That's why we organise intimate proposals, elegant birthdays, family anniversaries, business meetings and exceptional dinners — always with care for every detail, the atmosphere and the people who matter most.

What we create

Journeys full of emotion

I.

Private celebrations

Proposals, birthdays, anniversaries, jubilees, weddings, bachelor and bachelorette parties, family gatherings, private dinners and bespoke events. We design each one individually, with care for the atmosphere, the details and the emotions that stay with guests long after the event ends.

II.

A space for passionate people

We know how much heart and work go into every product or creation. We bring together people, inspiration and beautiful places, hosting gatherings where the passion of entrepreneurs and creators meets their audience — because the best promotion starts with genuine relationships.

III.

Corporate events

Team-building gatherings, company anniversaries, business dinners, product launches and client events. The best relationships are built outside the conference room — and a company's success is made by people who share exceptional moments together.

Why Tua Italia

Because to us, luxury isn't about excess. Luxury is time spent with the people you love. It's genuine emotion. It's conversation around a shared table. It's a sunset over the sea. It's the moments you want to return to.

Every event begins with a person. We get to know their story, listen to their dreams, and create experiences that stay with them for a lifetime.
